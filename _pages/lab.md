My research interests lie in **trustworthy machine learning**, with a focus on **fairness, robustness, and privacy**. 

---

## Current Projects

- **Fair Resource Allocation**  
  We develop methods to ensure that large language models and AI agents make fair, robust, and accountable decisions in high‑stakes resource allocation problems, including urban mobility, public safety, and disaster response. 
 
- **Trustworthy AI for Time‑Series and Event Forecasting**  
  We develop large‑scale models for time‑series and event‑sequence understanding, bridging LLM reasoning with structured temporal data. Applications include healthcare and safety‑critical monitoring, with a focus on interpretability and reliability.

- **Federated Learning and Edge AI**  
  We address distributed learning of large‑scale AI architectures (e.g., MoE) in resource‑constrained edge settings, jointly considering computation and communication to enable efficient and privacy‑aware deployment.
