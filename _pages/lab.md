My lab focuses on **trustworthy machine learning**, with a focus on **fairness, robustness, and privacy preservation**. 

---

## Current Projects

- **Fair Resource Allocation**  
   This thrust develops methods to ensure that large language models and agents make fair, robust, and accountable decisions in high‑stakes resource allocation, spanning urban mobility, predictive policing, and disaster response. 
 
- **Trustworthy AI for Time‑Series and Event Forecasting**  
  This thrust advances large‑scale models for time‑series and event‑sequence understanding, bridging LLM reasoning with structured temporal data. Applications include healthcare and safety‑critical monitoring, with a focus on interpretability and reliability.

- **Federated Learning and Edge AI**  
  This thrust addresses distributed learning of large‑scale AI architectures (e.g., MoE) in resource‑constrained edge settings, jointly considering computation and communication to enable efficient and privacy‑aware deployment.
